# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Standard Delivery

Standard orders are generally delivered within 3 to 7 business days.

## Order Tracking

Customers can check the current status of their order using their order number.

## Expected Delivery

The expected delivery date is calculated based on the order information available in the system.

## Delayed Orders

If an order has passed its expected delivery date, customers should contact customer support.

## Cancelled Orders

If an order is cancelled, customers can contact customer support to understand the reason for cancellation.

## Delivery Problems

Customers should create a support ticket if their order has not arrived or if there is a problem with delivery.

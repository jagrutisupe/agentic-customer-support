# Return and Refund Policy

## Return Window

Customers can request a return within 7 days of receiving the product.

## Eligibility

Products must be unused and returned in their original packaging.

The product should include all original accessories, manuals, and other items supplied with the order.

## Non-Returnable Items

Products that have been damaged by the customer or used beyond normal inspection may not be eligible for return.

## Refunds

Once the returned product is inspected and approved, the refund will be initiated.

Refund processing may take 5 to 7 business days depending on the payment method.

## Damaged Products

If a product arrives damaged, customers should contact customer support as soon as possible and provide the order details.

## Support

Customers can create a support ticket if they need assistance with a return or refund.

# Warranty Policy

## Product Warranty

Products may include a warranty period specified in the product information.

## Warranty Period

The warranty period can vary depending on the product.

Customers can check the warranty period associated with a specific product.

## Warranty Claims

Customers experiencing a problem with a product under warranty should contact customer support.

The customer may be asked to provide the order number and product information.

## Warranty Exclusions

Damage caused by misuse, accidental damage, unauthorized modifications, or improper handling may not be covered.

## Support

Customers can create a support ticket for assistance with warranty-related problems.
